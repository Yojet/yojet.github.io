Research

Current research

  1. Chromosome dynamics in trisomy 21 and across species

    Trisomy 21 causes Down syndrome. I am optimizing an image analysis pipeline that quantifies chromosome dynamics in human cells, in order to determine how an additional copy of chromosome 21 alters chromosome behavior.

  2. Comparative neuronal physiology across species

    I use patch-seq to record from stem cell-derived neurons of humans, chimpanzees and macaques, and compare their electrophysiological properties across species.

Proposed research

  1. Benchmarking iPSC-derived astrocytes

    Deriving astrocytes from iPSCs remains a challenge. I aim to benchmark current differentiation methods.

  2. Astrocyte regulation of neuronal physiology across species

    I aim to determine how human, chimpanzee and macaque astrocytes affect neuronal action potentials, spontaneous action potentials, endocytosis and neurotransmitter release.

  3. Immunology of the brain parenchyma

    I aim to define how the basic immunology of the brain parenchyma differs across species.

  4. Rates of mitosis across species

    I aim to image chromosomes across species and quantify the rate of mitosis.

Doctoral research

Lowe syndrome and OCRL

Lowe syndrome results from loss of OCRL, a phosphatase that dephosphorylates PI(4,5)P2. Patient-derived neural stem cells showed enhanced Notch-dependent gliogenesis, and the resulting neurons showed delayed physiological maturation. Inhibition of the lipid kinase PIP5K1C restored excitability and corrected the Notch defect in patient brain organoids.

Sharma et al., EMBO Molecular Medicine, 2025

Philosophy

What science means to me

There is an art to thinking about questions. A scientist can begin with one basic problem in one simple system, and if the question is deep enough, it keeps growing. It reaches new fields, new organisms and human disease, while staying rooted in the same fundamental biology.

I don't yet know which questions will define my career. I do know how I want to reach them. I want to start with something basic and foundational, and take the time to understand it. I want to follow a question into whichever system can answer it best, and keep more than one line of inquiry alive at once. Deep questions open doors no one could have planned for.
